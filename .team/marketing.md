# Marketing Audit

## Positioning

Buildy is "Polarsteps voor verbouwingen": document your renovation as it happens, share progress with followers, and turn the whole journey into a printed Bouwboek.

## Strong Messages

- "Van chaos in je camera roll naar een prachtig Bouwboek."
- "Leg elke sloopdag, mijlpaal en voor/na vast."
- "Deel je verbouwing zonder alles op Instagram kwijt te raken."

## Audience Segments

- Homeowners doing major renovations.
- Couples/families documenting a forever home.
- Interior designers and contractors who want client-facing progress logs.
- Real estate renovators who need before/after proof.

## Launch Assets Needed

- Three demo projects with complete timelines and Bouwboek previews.
- Before/after clips for short-form social.
- A sample printed Bouwboek or high-quality mockup photos.
- Landing-page section that explains the checkout/print flow simply.
