# Distribution Audit

## Organic Loops

- Public project pages invite followers.
- Each shared update can bring family/friends into Buildy.
- Printed Bouwboek becomes a physical referral artifact.

## Channels

- TikTok/Instagram Reels: before/after renovation stories.
- Pinterest: room transformations and Bouwboek spreads.
- SEO: renovation diary, photobook, remodel progress tracker.
- Partnerships: contractors, interior designers, architects, home improvement stores.
- Communities: Dutch renovation Facebook groups, Reddit renovation communities, homeowner forums.

## Product Distribution Improvements

- Add share cards for milestones and before/after moments.
- Add watermark/light branding to public shared project cards.
- Add "Maak ook je Bouwboek" CTA on public photobook/project pages for non-owners.
